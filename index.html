Everything you need to know about integrating Clara AI with your existing telephony, CRM, and sales pipelines.

Clara AI connects directly via SIP trunks, WebRTC, or native Twilio / Vonage integrations. It hooks into HubSpot, Salesforce, or custom webhooks via secure REST APIs, pulling product databases and pushing real-time conversation transcripts in milliseconds.

Standard voice bots route speech through generic multi-step HTTP endpoints, resulting in 1.5–3.0 second pauses. Clara uses streaming bidirectional audio buffers and edge neural TTS running on global PoPs, reducing end-to-end response turn-around to sub-450ms.
