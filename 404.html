The Origin of Clara AI

One Observation. One Question. One Mission.

Vitus Rodrigo Founder, ClaraAI

While visiting the UAE, I spent time with real estate sales teams and observed their customer conversations. I noticed something that kept coming up — sales reps often struggled when customers raised unexpected objections.

They knew their products, but when a customer said "The price is too high," "I need to think about it," or "I'm comparing other projects," they didn't always have the right response at that exact moment.

That made me think:

"What if every salesperson had an AI expert sitting beside them to help handle every customer call?"

When I returned to India, I started building ClaraAI — not to replace salespeople, but to give them real-time support during the moments that can make or break a deal.

And it doesn't stop with the salesperson. Managers can see exactly how each representative is performing, which objections they struggle with, how they handle conversations, and where coaching is needed.

My vision is simple: Help every salesperson handle objections with confidence and give every manager the intelligence to build a stronger sales team.

AI should never replace great salespeople. It should help every salesperson become great.

— Vitus Rodrigo, Founder ClaraAI
